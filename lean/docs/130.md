# Exact Fourier transforms below $`n\log n`$

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [An explicit power saving for the exact discrete Fourier transform](../../preprints/An-explicit-power-saving-for-the-exact-discrete-Fourier-transform-September-25-2026/main.pdf)
- [Finite tensor savings and exact Fourier circuits](../../preprints/Finite-tensor-savings-and-exact-Fourier-circuits-September-25-2026/main.pdf)

## Scope

The all-length algorithm in *An explicit power saving for the exact discrete Fourier transform* is not formalized here at this revision. The formalized result below comes from *Finite tensor savings and exact Fourier circuits*: it gives exact discrete Fourier transforms with arbitrarily small normalized circuit cost along an unbounded sequence of lengths. For every $c>0$ and every cutoff $N_0\ge2$, some $n\ge N_0$ has a circuit computing the unnormalized DFT with fewer than $cn\log_2 n$ gates. Addition, subtraction, and multiplication by a predetermined complex scalar each cost one gate; diagonal scalings are charged. The result is subsequential, with no all-length, bounded-coefficient, conditioning, or bit-complexity claim.

The module [PublishedParameters.lean](../OAI/Computability/FourierCircuit/PublishedParameters.lean) checks the displayed $h=100$ finite-network constants by exact integer and rational arithmetic. This certificate does not by itself formalize the network, the all-length recursion, or its running-time bound.

The reusable lemma [RecurrenceDepth.lean](../OAI/Computability/FourierCircuit/RecurrenceDepth.lean) proves a finite-depth bound for a divide-and-conquer recurrence. Instantiating its abstract depth and recurrence hypotheses with the all-length Fourier construction remains future work.

The exploratory [CenterCompression.lean](../OAI/Computability/FourierCircuit/CenterCompression.lean)
proves the characteristic-zero scalar identity behind a proposed removal of the
global centre coordinate: for every three-element target set, subtracting one
third of the global sum from each of the three centre entries gives exactly the
same readout after summing and dividing by two. It also checks the resulting
`h = 24` constants. This is an algebraic certificate only; the frame-labelled
network and its directional-step accounting are not yet formalized.

The accompanying [FrameBudget.lean](../OAI/Computability/FourierCircuit/FrameBudget.lean)
formalizes the arithmetic interface expected from that frame proof: source and
sink dimensions telescope, and every internal decrease contributes twice its
dimension to the absolute residual budget. Its H=24 instance checks the
compressed residual total and saving. The subspace nesting and orthonormal
residual-basis obligations remain explicit hypotheses, so this module does not
claim to complete the binary-frame construction.

The smaller [FrameLabels.lean](../OAI/Computability/FourierCircuit/FrameLabels.lean)
certificate checks the local binary facts used by the side-wire edge: triple
indicator dot products are intersection parities over `ZMod 2`, even
intersection gives line inclusion in the relevant orthogonal complement, and a
three-element indicator line is nondegenerate. Tensor-product labels and the
full eight-row network still require a separate development.

The conditional [SideMergeObstruction.lean](../OAI/Computability/FourierCircuit/SideMergeObstruction.lean)
certificate records the arithmetic consequence of charging two residual units
for every extra incomparable neighbour incidence. At H=24 that penalty is 4176
times the original `2*N0` margin, so such a grouping cannot preserve a positive
saving. The premise is geometric and conditional; it is not a lower bound for
all possible future frame designs.

The [ImprovementComparison.lean](../OAI/Computability/FourierCircuit/ImprovementComparison.lean)
module compares the uncompressed and centre-compressed H=24 multipliers by
exact rational arithmetic. It proves a strict decrease of the recurrence
multiplier by `576081/34359738368`; this is a verified finite-certificate
improvement, pending the remaining frame-labelled circuit proof.

The [JoinPenalty.lean](../OAI/Computability/FourierCircuit/JoinPenalty.lean)
module proves the finite-dimensional geometric core of the side-wire audit: a
common subspace containing two disjoint nonzero lines has dimension at least
two, hence two line-removal charges already cost at least two units. This is a
conditional local obstruction, not a lower bound for every possible circuit.

The [TensorFrameLabels.lean](../OAI/Computability/FourierCircuit/TensorFrameLabels.lean)
module represents the triple tensor address space concretely as functions on a
product of three finite coordinates. It proves pure-tensor dot-product
factorization, unit norm for triple bank entries, and orthogonality whenever a
coordinate pair has even intersection. This establishes the local
nondegenerate line labels used by the neighbour edge; the remaining
tensor-product complement labels and the full eight-row network are still
open.
